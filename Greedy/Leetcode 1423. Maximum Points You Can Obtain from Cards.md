## Variable Pattern

| Name | Meaning |
|---|---|
| cardPoints | points on each card in the row |
| k | cards to take from the two ends |
| n | number of cards |
| total | points of the current choice of k end cards |
| res | best total found |
| i | how many cards have moved from the left end to the right end |
